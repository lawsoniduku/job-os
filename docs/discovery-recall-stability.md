# Recover relevant jobs without increasing database admission limits

The intake filter used a coarse regional tag before the authoritative eligibility engine. Explicit EMEA locations could be discarded because company marketing mentioned Kenya or South Africa. A contradictory `is_remote=false` flag could also discard a worldwide remote location before the engine read the posting. Recovery now defers those conflicts to the existing engine. Explicit foreign residency and physical attendance keep their original outcomes; no country eligibility rule or inference version changes.

New intake uses a bounded newest-publication-first buffer, at most the configured 500 rows and 10 MiB, rather than the first rows in upstream file order. Download/creation timestamps do not promote undated jobs. Existing owned openings still refresh while new intake is zero or full. Deterministic admission precedes content deduplication, so an ineligible requisition cannot hide a separate eligible sibling. Unnamed companies do not collapse into one content key. The CSV is still streamed; only the bounded selected descriptions remain in memory. A partial download produces explicit failure and can retain its bounded partial progress.

A real production Trafilea performance-and-talent development vacancy was present but rejected by a performance-management query. Narrow combined HR title aliases now cover this and combined people-and-talent operations titles. Marketing, computing and recruiting counterexamples remain excluded. These are role taxonomy changes only; geographic confidence is unchanged.

Adversarial regressions failed before fixes: fresh tail job lost to older first row, ineligible sibling poisoning dedup, fresh duplicate losing to old requisition, per-candidate reads with a zero budget, EMEA/remote flag gate losses, and the combined HR title losses. A 2,000-row reversed feed fixture proves the same newest three survive both traversal orders with a bounded payload.
